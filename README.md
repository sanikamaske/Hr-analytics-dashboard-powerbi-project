# 📊 HR Analytics Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This interactive Power BI dashboard delivers a deep-dive analysis into employee attrition, workforce demographics, and organizational KPIs. It enables HR leadership to pinpoint turnover trends, identify at-risk job roles, and formulate data-driven employee retention strategies.

## 🔍 Data Insights & Breakdown

### 1. Demographics & Education
* **Age Group:** Employees aged **26–35** display the highest attrition volume compared to any other age bracket[cite: 3].
* **Education Field:** Staff with **Life Sciences (37%)** and **Medical (29%)** education backgrounds account for over two-thirds of total attrition[cite: 3].

### 2. Job Roles & Departments
* **High-Turnover Roles:** **Laboratory Technicians (62)**, **Sales Executives (57)**, and **Research Scientists (47)** suffer from the highest rate of employee departures[cite: 3].
* **Gender Ratio:** Male employees exhibit a higher overall attrition count than female employees across key business units[cite: 3].

### 3. Financial & Tenure Impact
* **Salary Slab:** Attrition is heavily concentrated among workforce members in lower compensation bands[cite: 3].
* **Tenure:** Turnover spikes during the initial years of service, stabilizing as tenure increases[cite: 3].
